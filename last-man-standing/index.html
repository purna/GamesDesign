Last Man Standing

Top-down arena battle royale. The arena is a 5x5 grid of rooms linked by doors — you only ever see your own room. Collect weapons & shields (you can stack multiple), grab a map to reveal the arena for 10 seconds, dodge roaming enemies, and be the last one standing — attacking and defending only start burning down their timers once you actually use them. A room locks once 2 players are inside, and unlocks when one leaves or dies. New arrivals within the same 60-second window are grouped into one arena; once a match starts it runs until there's a winner, then everyone moves into a fresh arena — up to 12 players per arena.
