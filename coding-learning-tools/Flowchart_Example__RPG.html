Explore game logic, build your own flowchart, check the paths and reflect on your work.

1. Explore the worked examples

A flowchart shows the order of actions and decisions. Follow the arrows from Start. A decision has Yes and No paths.

2. Build your own flowchart

Each flowchart begins with one permanent Start. Add actions, decisions and one or more Ends, or create an example. Choose where each arrow goes. Use a question for each decision. Drag a step using its drag handle, or move it up or down with the buttons. Drop above or below another step to change the layout; arrow connections stay connected. Select a step heading to minimise or expand it.

Each named flowchart saves automatically on this device, including its reflection.

3. Check your game logic
Trace one path where the answer is Yes, then one where it is No. If a path loops, explain what changes before you try again.
    Ask a partner: can they follow your arrows without you explaining them?
