    3. Review your own game (30 mins)
    

    Use the builder above to draw a class diagram for your own game, for example a Player with Movement and Health, or a Monster with variants.
    

    • Give your Character class 4 to 6 attributes with types and visibility.
    • Add 2 to 3 methods, for example Move(), Attack(), Die().
    • Link it to at least one other class (Inventory, Weapon, Party...) and label the multiplicity.
    • Write the C# for your class: fields, a constructor if you used composition, and method stubs.
    • Check: did you use inheritance, and composition or aggregation, in the right place?
